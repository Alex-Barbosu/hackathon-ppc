Figures from ANRE's annual performance-indicator reports, as reported in the press.

**National SAIDI (minutes per customer per year)**
- 2019: 350 total = 171 planned + 179 unplanned. About 9.5 million customers
  (5.2 M urban, 4.3 M rural).
- 2022: down 46 minutes on 2021.
- 2023: about 238. 2024: 229 (131 planned, 98 unplanned).
- 2025: 236 (145 planned, 91 unplanned): the first increase in several years, driven by
  planned work; about 10.3 million customers connected.

**Benchmark.** ANRE compared 2019 with advanced European countries: planned 40–150 and
unplanned 20–100 minutes a year. Romania was about twice as long overall.

**Operators (2019 unplanned SAIDI, minutes).** e-Distribuție Banat 227 (highest of the eight
concession DSOs), Oltenia 204, Delgaz Grid 188, Transilvania Sud 186, Muntenia Nord 179,
Transilvania Nord 149, e-Distribuție Dobrogea 147, e-Distribuție Muntenia 142.8 (lowest).
Muntenia also had the lowest planned SAIDI (55 min); Delgaz the highest (288 min). The three
e-Distribuție companies are today's Rețele Electrice Banat, Dobrogea and Muntenia (PPC group).
In 2025 unplanned SAIDI excluding weather, customer and third-party causes ranged
66.7 (Delgaz Grid) to 109 (Oltenia) minutes.

**How the analyst uses this**
- Use these as yardsticks only; FileGrid's outage data is simulated. Say so.
- Planned outages are a large share of Romanian SAIDI. An outage hotspot analysis must split
  planned from unplanned before drawing conclusions about network health.
- Among the PPC areas, Banat historically had the worst unplanned SAIDI and Muntenia the best;
  a hotspot map that agrees is plausible, one that strongly disagrees deserves a check.
