Rețele Electrice Dobrogea's 2024 continuity indicators (ANRE annex 2), split by area, voltage
level and cause. Units are the usual ones (SAIDI minutes, SAIFI interruptions, per customer).

**Planned interruptions.** SAIDI rural LV 422 min, rural MV 230; urban LV 52.5, urban MV 54.6.
SAIFI rural LV 1.55 vs urban LV 0.29.

**Unplanned, caused by the DSO.** SAIDI rural MV 113, rural LV 68; urban MV 14.9, urban LV 5.2.

**Unplanned, users or third parties.** SAIDI rural MV 108, rural LV 99.6; urban MV 56, urban LV 31.

**Other unplanned (incl. weather).** SAIDI rural MV 152, rural LV 122, rural HV 84; urban LV 50,
urban MV 40.

**Momentary (MAIFI).** Rural LV 25.0, rural MV 21.9; urban MV 5.7, urban LV 5.4.

**Reading it**
- Rural customers see more interruption minutes than urban ones in every cause category:
  about 2.4× for weather and other causes, 3× for third parties, 8× for planned work and up
  to 13× for DSO-caused LV faults. Long overhead rural feeders are exposed to wind, ice and
  vegetation and take longer to reach.
- MAIFI is about 4× higher in rural areas: many brief faults cleared by reclosers. High MAIFI
  often predicts later sustained faults on the same feeder.
- Planned work is the largest single block in rural LV (422 minutes).

**How the analyst uses this**
- Never rank hotspots on raw event counts: normalise per customer and split rural/urban and
  planned/unplanned, or the map just shows where most customers live.
- A county that is mostly rural should be compared with rural yardsticks, not urban ones.
